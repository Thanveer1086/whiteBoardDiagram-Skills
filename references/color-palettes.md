## Usage Tips

1. **One palette per board** — don't mix palettes; pick one and stick with it
2. **Use fills for areas, borders for emphasis** — fills are always the lighter variant
3. **Reserve warm colors** (pink, orange, yellow) for attention-grabbing sections
4. **Cool colors** (blue, green, purple) work best for technical/data sections
5. **Consistent border colors** — always use the accent variant from the same palette
6. **White/light backgrounds** — whiteboard diagrams look best on `#FFFFFF` or `#FAFAFA`
